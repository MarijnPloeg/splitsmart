THE RESTAURANT BILL, SOLVED

Scan the bill.
Everyone taps what they had.

Point your camera at the receipt. SplitSmart reads every dish, price and the tip, mostly right on your iPhone. Tap what you had, tap a friend’s face to share a dish, and the tip is shared fairly. A guest at the table can join for that one bill. Anything left over? SplitSmart asks “Whose was this?” before the bill goes into the group.

Everything a group actually needs

The everyday stuff stays simple. The handy extras are there when you need them and out of the way when you don't.

Any receipt

Restaurant bills, shop receipts and delivery screenshots from Uber Eats, Thuisbezorgd or any app. Or send one from anywhere with Share → SplitSmart.

Trips, any currency

Pay in rand, dollars or pounds. Each expense is converted once, with your card’s amount or the ECB rate of the day. Car rides split by the kilometre.

Homes and couples

One quiet line shows whose turn it is for the groceries. Pay with Apple Pay and SplitSmart offers to split it, right at the till (set up once in Shortcuts).

People

Everyone you split with in one place, and where you stand with each. Add a friend from Contacts: SplitSmart only sees the person you pick, and nothing is uploaded.

Insights, just for you

Your food profile, your tips per country and how a trip is going against your own budget. Worked out on your iPhone, never compared, never sold.

Receipts for bookkeepers

Send your share with the original receipt and the VAT per rate. Or export a period as PDFs with an overview spreadsheet.

SETTLE UP

Trip over?
All square in a few payments.

Close the trip and SplitSmart works out the fewest payments that settle everyone. Pay with a payment link, a QR code or by copying an IBAN, then tap Paid. Pay in rand, settle in euros, at your card's real rate. When the last payment is in: Clean Slate.

CALM, NOT NAGGY

Staying tidy is rewarded. Spending more never is.

A group Tidy score, badges for quick claims and fast settling that grow from Silver to Gold to Diamond, and fun facts about your group’s habits. Your badges show on your page for the people in your groups, and tiers never go down. No leaderboards, no guilt trips. Reminders come from Splitty, not from your friends.
