The restaurant bill, solved

Scan the bill.
Everyone taps what they had.

Point your camera at the receipt. SplitSmart reads every dish, price and the tip, mostly right on your iPhone. Tap what you had, tap a friend’s face to share a dish, and the tip is shared fairly. Anything left over? SplitSmart asks “Who had it?” before the bill goes into the group.

Get paid back

Friends pay you their way.

Add how you like to be paid once, under Get paid to. When it’s time, friends see your details with the amount already filled in where the app allows it. A guest at the table gets a link and can pay without the app. They tap “I’ve paid”, you tap “It’s in”.

Everything a group actually needs

The everyday stuff stays simple. The handy extras are there when you need them and out of the way when you don’t.

Any receipt

Restaurant bills, shop slips and delivery screenshots. Or send one from any app with Share → SplitSmart. Can’t read it? Just split the total.

Trips, any currency

Pay in rand, dollars or pounds. Each expense is converted once, with your card’s amount or the ECB rate of the day. Car rides split by the kilometre.

A rented house

Split the rent by the nights each person stays. Monthly payments in the house’s currency, and someone joining later just changes the shares.

Groceries, per meal

Keep a shop slip for later and split it per meal: what you ate and who ate it. The rest goes into the group when you wrap up.

Homes and couples

One quiet line shows whose turn it is for the groceries. Paid with Apple Pay in a shop? SplitSmart can offer to split it, and you confirm (set up once in Shortcuts).

People

Everyone you split with in one place, and where you stand with each. Add a friend from Contacts: SplitSmart only sees the person you pick.

Insights, just for you

Your food profile, your tips per country and how a trip is going against your own plan. Worked out on your iPhone, never compared, never sold.

Receipts for bookkeepers

Send your share with the original receipt and the VAT per rate. Or export a period as PDFs with an overview spreadsheet.

Settle up

Trip over?
All square in a few payments.

Close the trip and SplitSmart works out the fewest payments that settle everyone. Pay with a link, a QR code or an IBAN, then tap Paid. Pay in rand, settle in euros, at your card’s real rate. When the last payment is in: Clean Slate.

Calm, not naggy

Staying tidy is rewarded. Spending more never is.

A group Tidy score, and badges for quick claims and fast settling that grow from Bronze to Silver, Gold and Diamond. Tiers never go down. No leaderboards, no guilt trips, and reminders come from Splitty, not from your friends.

  • Widgets for your balance, your trip and what’s left to do

  • Siri and Shortcuts: “What do I owe in SplitSmart?”
  • Scan from the Action button, Control Center or the Lock Screen
  • A notification when a bill is ready or someone pays you back
