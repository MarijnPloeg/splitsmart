Privacy Policy

In short

SplitSmart helps friends split shared costs. We only use your data to make that work. We don't show ads, don't track you across apps, and never sell your data. Your data is stored in the EU, and you can delete your account in the app at any time.

1. Who is responsible

SplitSmart is made by Marijn Ploeg in the Netherlands, who is the data controller for the app. For any privacy question or request, email marijnploeg@gmail.com.

2. What we collect and why

DataWhy we use it
Sign in with AppleTo create your account. We receive an Apple user identifier and, if you choose to share them, your name and email (which can be an Apple relay address).
ProfileYour display name, an optional profile photo and your language, so your friends recognise you.
Groups and expensesGroups you create or join, their members, expenses, splits, settlements and notes. This is the core of the app, shared only with members of the same group.
Receipts and screenshotsPhotos of bills and screenshots of delivery orders that you choose to scan, and the items read from them, so everyone can claim what they had.
Payment detailsOptional: your IBAN, account holder name or payment link, shown only to members of your groups so they can pay you back.
Guests on a billA first name you or a guest enters for one bill, and that guest's share. Guests don't need an account.
Bank transactions (optional)Only if you connect a bank: card payments from the accounts you choose, used to suggest expenses to split. Read-only. You confirm every suggestion.
Device tokenTo send you notifications you asked for, such as a bill being ready to claim.

We do not collect your location, contacts, health data or advertising identifiers, and we do not use analytics or tracking tools.

3. Legal basis

We process your data to provide the service you signed up for (performance of a contract, GDPR article 6(1)(b)). Connecting a bank and enabling notifications are based on your consent (article 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time in the app or in iOS Settings.

4. Who processes data for us

  • Supabase hosts our database, file storage and server functions in the EU (Frankfurt, Germany).
  • Apple provides Sign in with Apple and delivers push notifications.
  • Anthropic reads a receipt image only when your iPhone cannot read it on the device. The image is sent to Anthropic's API to extract the items and amounts. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, this data is not used to train their models.
  • Enable Banking (Finland) connects to your bank under PSD2 when you choose to connect a bank account.
  • Frankfurter provides public ECB exchange rates. No personal data is sent.

Most receipts are read entirely on your iPhone and never leave it for this purpose. When data is processed outside the EU, it is protected by the safeguards the GDPR requires, such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

5. Who can see your data

Members of a group see that group's expenses, receipts, balances, names and photos, and the payment details you chose to add. People outside your groups see nothing, except that a guest scanning a bill's QR code sees that one bill's items and the first names of the people on it.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your data while you have an account. When you delete your account in the app (Profile, Delete account), your profile, photo, payment details, device tokens and badges are deleted. Expenses you shared with a group stay visible to the other members under the name "Deleted user", so their balances remain correct. Bank connections expire automatically after the consent period your bank allows (usually 90 or 180 days) or when you disconnect.

7. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, export or delete your data, or object to or restrict how we use it. Most of this you can do in the app; for anything else, email marijnploeg@gmail.com and we'll respond within one month. You can also file a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens, autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl).

8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit, access is restricted per group in the database, and server keys never reach the app. No system is perfectly secure, and we will inform you if a breach affects your data.

9. Children

SplitSmart is not meant for children under 16.

10. Changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we'll tell you in the app before the change takes effect.
