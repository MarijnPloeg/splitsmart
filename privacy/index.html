In short

SplitSmart helps friends split shared costs. We only use your data to make that work. We don't show ads, don't track you across apps, and never sell your data. Your data is stored in the EU, and you can delete your account in the app at any time. When you use Apple Pay in SplitSmart, the payment is handled by Stripe and Apple: we never see or store your card.

1. Who is responsible

SplitSmart is made by Marijn Ploeg in the Netherlands, who is the data controller for the app and this website. For any privacy question or request, email marijnploeg@gmail.com.

3. Usage statistics

To see which features help and where people get stuck, the app counts how it is used with TelemetryDeck, a privacy-first analytics service hosted in the EU. It receives events such as "a group was created" or "a bill was split", with simple labels (the kind of group, how a bill was read, about how many people), plus the app version, iOS version, device model and language.

We never send names, amounts, merchants, receipt contents, anything you type, or your account. Events carry a random number made on your iPhone for this purpose only, which is hashed on your iPhone and again on TelemetryDeck's server, so they can't be linked to you. No IP address is stored, nothing is used for advertising or tracking, and nothing is sold.

You can turn this off at any time in the app: Profile, App, "Share anonymous usage stats". It then stops at once, in the app, its App Clip and its share extension. This website counts visits with TelemetryDeck's cookieless script, without cookies or personal data, and only on the home, privacy, terms and support pages. The invite, table and pay pages never load it.

4. Card payments through Stripe (when you use Apple Pay in SplitSmart)

Paying a share with Apple Pay is being prepared and is not yet available to everyone. This section explains how it works when you use it.

How it works. Payments are processed by Stripe (Stripe Technology Europe Ltd and its affiliates) through Stripe Connect. The person who is paid has their own Stripe account; SplitSmart starts the payment and Stripe sends the money to that account. SplitSmart never holds the money and never sees card details.

If you pay with Apple Pay

  • Your card and the Apple Pay payment token are handled by Apple and Stripe. SplitSmart never receives or stores your card number or the token.
  • Stripe receives what it needs to process the payment and prevent fraud, such as the payment token, the amount and a short description of what it is for (for example the bill or group name). Stripe may collect information about your device for fraud prevention.
  • SplitSmart stores the amount, the service fee, the status and Stripe's reference for the payment, so both of you can see it went through.

If you receive payments

  • To receive card payments you set up a Stripe account inside SplitSmart (Get paid to, Apple Pay & cards). Stripe asks who you are and collects the identity and bank details it needs to verify you and pay you out, as required by law. Stripe decides what it needs and keeps it under its own policy.
  • To save you typing, SplitSmart fills in what it already knows: your name (from the account holder name you entered), your email, your IBAN from Get paid to, and a short description of what the account is for (receiving shares of shared bills from friends). You can change all of it in Stripe's form.
  • SplitSmart stores only the reference of your Stripe account and whether it can receive payments and payouts. Your identity documents and verification results stay with Stripe.

Stripe as a separate company

Stripe processes payment data as an independent controller for its own legal duties (such as identity checks, anti-money-laundering rules and fraud prevention), and as our processor for the payments themselves. Read Stripe's privacy policy. Someone receiving payments also agrees to the Stripe Connected Account Agreement.

Service fee, refunds and disputes. The person paying sees a small service fee before paying; the person being paid gets their full share. The fee and how refunds and disputes are handled are explained in the Terms of Use.

5. Legal basis

We process your data to provide the service you signed up for (performance of a contract, GDPR article 6(1)(b)), including card payments you choose to make or receive. Notifications are based on your consent (article 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time in iOS Settings. Anonymous usage statistics rely on our legitimate interest in improving the app (article 6(1)(f)); you can switch them off in Profile. Some payment records are kept because the law requires it (article 6(1)(c)).

6. Who processes data for us

  • Supabase hosts our database, file storage and server functions in the EU (Frankfurt, Germany).
  • Apple provides Sign in with Apple, Apple Pay and push notifications. If you use Siri, Apple handles your voice request under its own privacy policy.
  • Anthropic reads a receipt image only when your iPhone cannot read it on the device. The image is sent to Anthropic's API to extract the items and amounts. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, this data is not used to train their models.
  • Stripe processes card payments when you use Apple Pay in SplitSmart, and verifies people who receive them (section 4).
  • TelemetryDeck (Germany) receives the anonymous usage statistics described in section 3, stored in the EU.
  • Frankfurter provides public ECB exchange rates. No personal data is sent.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website. Like any web host it processes your IP address to deliver the pages.

Most receipts are read entirely on your iPhone and never leave it for this purpose. When data is processed outside the EU (for example by Anthropic or Stripe in the United States), it is protected by the safeguards the GDPR requires, such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified under it.

7. Who can see your data

Members of a group see that group's expenses, receipts, balances, names and photos, and the payment details you chose to add. People you share a group with can also see the badges you earned, with their tiers and counts, on your page in the app; your insights and trip budgets stay on your iPhone and only you see them. People outside your groups see nothing, except that a guest scanning a bill's table QR code sees that one bill's items and the first names of the people on it, and a guest you send a pay link to sees their share, your name and the payment details you added. People in the app only lists people you already share a group with.

8. How long we keep it

We keep your data while you have an account. When you delete your account in the app (Profile, Delete account), your profile, photo, payment details, device tokens and badges are deleted. Expenses you shared with a group stay visible to the other members under the name "Verwijderde gebruiker" (deleted user), so their balances remain correct.

Receipt photos, screenshots and house documents are kept while their group exists. For your bookkeeping or an expense claim, send a copy to your bookkeeper and keep it in your own records.

Records of card payments are kept as long as the law requires for payment and tax records. If you received card payments, deleting your SplitSmart account removes our link to your Stripe account, but the Stripe account itself and its payout history stay with Stripe; you can close it with Stripe.

9. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, export or delete your data, or object to or restrict how we use it. Most of this you can do in the app; for anything else, email marijnploeg@gmail.com and we'll respond within one month. For data Stripe holds as a controller, you can also contact Stripe directly. You can file a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens, autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl).

10. Security

Data is encrypted in transit, access is restricted per group in the database, and server keys never reach the app. Payment keys stay on our server and with Stripe. No system is perfectly secure, and we will inform you if a breach affects your data.
