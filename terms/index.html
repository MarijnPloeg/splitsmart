In short

SplitSmart is a free tool that keeps track of who paid what and helps friends pay each other back. It is not a bank. Scans and suggestions only propose: you confirm every split and every payment. Check the amounts before you pay, and be kind to the people in your groups.

1. The service

SplitSmart lets you record shared expenses, split them, scan receipts, see who owes whom and share how you'd like to be paid back. The app is free. Features may change, and the service may occasionally be unavailable.

2. Not a bank

SplitSmart does not hold or guarantee money, and is not a bank, payment institution or financial adviser. Most payments happen outside the app: friends pay you with your IBAN, a payment QR code or a payment link (such as bunq.me, Revolut, PayPal.me, SnapScan or Tikkie) in their own bank or payment app, and you mark it as paid. When you use Apple Pay in SplitSmart, the payment is processed by Stripe, a licensed payment provider, as described in section 3.

3. Card payments through Stripe (when you use Apple Pay in SplitSmart)

Paying a share with Apple Pay is being prepared and is not yet available to everyone. When it is, these rules apply.

  • Who processes it. Card payments are processed by Stripe through Stripe Connect. The money goes from the payer's card through Stripe to the Stripe account of the person being paid; SplitSmart never holds it. SplitSmart is the platform that starts the payment.
  • Receiving payments. To receive card payments you set up a Stripe account in the app and agree to the Stripe Connected Account Agreement, which includes the Stripe Services Agreement. Stripe verifies who you are and pays out to your bank account on its schedule. Stripe may ask for more information, pause payouts or refuse an account under its own rules.
  • The service fee. The person paying pays a service fee on top of the share. It is currently 5% of the share, at least €1.49 and at most €3.99 (other currencies have similar limits, such as £1.29 to £3.49, $1.69 to $4.49 and R30 to R79). The fee is always shown before you pay. The person being paid receives the full share; the fee goes to SplitSmart and covers Stripe's costs.
  • Only for sharing costs. Card payments are meant for paying friends back for shared costs. Don't use them to sell goods or services, for gifts of money or for anything illegal.
  • Refunds. A card payment is only made when you confirm it with Apple Pay. If a payment can't be used, for example because a guest changed their dishes after paying, SplitSmart refunds it in full, fee included, to the card. Other refunds you settle between yourselves.
  • Disputes. If a payment is disputed with the card issuer or refunded, the payment is marked as not paid in SplitSmart again, so the share is open between you once more. SplitSmart handles the dispute with Stripe.
  • Problems. If a card payment didn't arrive or looks wrong, email us. We'll look into it with Stripe.

4. Check the numbers

Receipt scanning, currency conversion and suggestions are automated and can be wrong. They only suggest: nothing is saved, split or paid until you confirm it. You are responsible for checking amounts before saving or paying them. Exchange rates come from the European Central Bank or from the amount you enter. Insights and trip budgets are for your own information only and never change a split.

5. Your account and content

  • Only add other people's names, photos or payment details when you have a good reason to and they would expect it. Only add payment details that are yours.
