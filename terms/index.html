Terms of Use

In short

SplitSmart is a free tool that keeps track of who paid what. It is not a bank and doesn't move money. Check the amounts before you pay, and be kind to the people in your groups.

1. The service

SplitSmart lets you record shared expenses, split them, scan receipts and see who owes whom. The app is currently free. Features may change, and the service may occasionally be unavailable.

2. Not a financial service

SplitSmart does not hold, transfer or guarantee money, and is not a bank, payment institution or financial adviser. Payments happen outside the app, through your own bank or payment app. Bank connections are read-only and only suggest expenses, and Apple Pay suggestions and Shortcuts actions ask before they save anything; you decide what is saved.

3. Check the numbers

Receipt scanning, currency conversion and suggestions are automated and can be wrong. You are responsible for checking amounts before saving or paying them. Exchange rates come from the European Central Bank or from the amount you enter. Insights and trip budgets are for your own information only and never change a split.

4. Your account and content

  • You keep ownership of what you add, such as expenses, photos and notes. You give us permission to store and show it to the members of your groups, only to run the service.
  • Only add other people's names, photos or payment details when you have a good reason to and they would expect it.
